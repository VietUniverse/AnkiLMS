Mastering TOPIK II Task 54: Elite Essay Writing Strategies & High-Yield Syntax | Super Real Korean

TOPIK II Writing Task 54 requires a formal, highly logical essay structure consisting of clear Introduction, Body, and Conclusion sections within 600–700 characters.

Employing advanced connectors and formal academic idioms (~을/를 막론하고, ~에 발맞춰) immediately elevates your lexical scoring band into Levels 5 and 6.

Constructing tight argumentative frameworks using cause-and-effect syntax (~을/를 토대로, ~는 반면) ensures structural coherence and persuasive depth.

Using authoritative discourse markers (이러한 맥락에서, 간과할 수 없는 사실은) signals strong critical thinking and journalistic writing precision.

## 📚 High-Yield Vocabulary Masterclass for Essay Writing

Integrate these 3 formal, high-yield academic phrases into your Intro and Conclusion sections to command top evaluation marks.

## 1. ~을/를 막론하고 (~eul/reul mang-non-ha-go)

Definition
Regardless of ~; Irrespective of ~; Without exception.

Formal Counterparts
~을/를 불문하고 (Regardless of), ~에 관계없이 (Irrespective of)

Contextual Application

남녀노소를 막론하고 현대 사회의 모든 구성원은 환경 문제의 심각성을 인지해야 한다.

Regardless of age or gender, all members of modern society must recognize the severity of environmental issues.

## 2. ~에 발맞춰 (~e bal-mat-chwo)

Definition
In step with ~; Keeping pace with ~; Aligning with ongoing shifts.

Formal Counterparts
~에 부응하여 (In response to), ~의 흐름에 따라 (Following the trend of)

Contextual Application

급격한 시대의 변화에 발맞춰 기존의 교육 제도와 제반 법률 역시 개혁되어야 한다.

In step with rapid technological shifts, existing educational systems and regulations must be reformed.

## 3. 난제 (nan-je)

Definition
An intractable problem; A complex dilemma requiring systemic intervention.

Structural Pairings
난제를 해결하다 (Resolve a dilemma), 난제에 봉착하다 (Confront a intractable problem)

Contextual Application

저출생 고령화 현상은 우리 사회가 조속히 해결해야 할 대표적인 난제 이다.

Low birthrates and an aging population represent a paramount intractable problem our society must urgently resolve.

## ✍️ Advanced Syntax Structural Blueprint for Essay Body

Construct cohesive argumentative body paragraphs by deploying logical cause, contrast, and evidentiary syntax patterns.

## 💡 1. ~을/를 토대로 (~eul/reul to-dae-ro)

Functional Blueprint: Establishes that the preceding concept serves as the foundational evidence, data, or principle for the subsequent claim.

실전 예시: 객관적인 기존의 연구 결과를 토대로 새로운 정책적 대안을 제시하는 것이 타당하다.

English Translation: It is appropriate to propose new policy alternatives based on objective existing research outcomes.

## 💡 2. ~는 반면 (~neun ban-myeon)

Functional Blueprint: Sharpens essay argumentation by juxtaposing two directly contrasting facts or outcomes within a single complex sentence.

실전 예시: 디지털 기술의 발전으로 정보 접근성이 비약적으로 높아진 반면 , 정보의 신뢰성은 심각하게 저하되고 있다.

English Translation: While information accessibility has dramatically increased due to digital advancements, information reliability is severely declining.

## 💡 3. 이러한 맥락에서 (i-reo-han maek-rak-e-seo)

Functional Blueprint: Connects preceding background context directly to your main thesis statement or solution proposal.

실전 예시: 이러한 맥락에서 문제 해결을 위한 정부와 시민사회의 자발적인 협업이 무엇보다 절실하다.

English Translation: In this context, voluntary collaboration between government and civil society is urgently needed.

## 💡 4. 간과할 수 없는 사실은 ~ (gan-gwa-hal su eom-neun sa-sil-eun)

Functional Blueprint: Emphasizes a pivotal factor that opponents or general readers frequently overlook, giving your essay editorial authority.

실전 예시: 간과할 수 없는 사실은 급격한 경제 성장의 그늘에 심각한 계층 간 양극화가 존재한다는 점이다.

English Translation: An undeniable fact that cannot be overlooked is that severe socio-economic polarization exists in the shadow of rapid economic growth.
