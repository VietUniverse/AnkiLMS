## What Is on the TOPIK Writing Section?

The TOPIK writing section has four tasks that grow from completing two sentences to writing a full argumentative essay. Writing is worth as much as listening or reading, a third of the TOPIK II total, but it has only 4 questions against 50 in each of the other sections. Each writing question therefore carries far more points than any multiple-choice item.

## What a Question 51 Blank Looks Like

Question 51 gives a short practical text with two gaps, ㉠ and ㉡, and you write one sentence for each that fits both the meaning and the style. Here is a short example written in the same pattern:

이번 주 토요일에 저희 집에서 집들이를 합니다 . 시간이 있으면 ( ㉠ ). 집 근처에 주차장이 없습니다 . 그러니까 ( ㉡ ).

"We're having a housewarming at our place this Saturday. If you have time, ( ㉠ ). There is no car park near the house. So ( ㉡ )."

Good answers are ㉠ 꼭 와 주세요 (kkok wa juseyo, "please be sure to come") and ㉡ 대중교통을 이용해 주세요 (daejunggyotongeul iyonghae juseyo, "please use public transport"). 집들이 (jipdeuri, "housewarming") is said [ 집뜨리 ]. The second gap is answered by the sentence before it: no parking, so take public transport. Reading the sentences around each gap is the core skill of questions 51 and 52.

## The Written Style for Questions 53 and 54

Questions 53 and 54 are marked partly on register, and graders expect the written plain style ( 한다체 , handache), which ends sentences in - 다 . A sentence you would say as 요즘 1 인 가구가 늘고 있어요 (yojeum irin gaguga neulgo isseoyo) ("single-person households are increasing these days") becomes 최근 1 인 가구가 늘고 있다 (choegeun irin gaguga neulgo itda) in an answer. 1 인 (one person) is read 일인 , said [ 이린 ]. Polite - 요 endings do not change the meaning, but they are the wrong register for a report or an essay. ## The Four Questions

TOPIK question 51 . Filling two blanks, ㉠ and ㉡, in a practical text such as a notice, email or advert, keeping the text's own polite style.

TOPIK question 52 . Completing a short explanatory passage in the plain - 다 style, using connectors such as 그러나 and 따라서 to find what each blank must do.

TOPIK question 53 . Describing a chart or survey in 200-300 characters with a four-part frame: what was surveyed, the main results, a comparison and a summary.

TOPIK 54 essay . Planning and writing a 600-700 character opinion essay that answers every guiding question, with a timed plan and an annotated model answer.

## How Should You Split the 50 Minutes?

Split the 50 minutes roughly in proportion to the points: about 5 minutes each for questions 51 and 52, 15 for question 53 and 25 for question 54. That budget gives every question about two points per minute, so no task is starved. The plan is editorial, not an official rule.

Two adjustments help most candidates. First, if you aim for Level 3, make sure questions 51 to 53 are complete before you start question 54; they carry half the points and are easier to finish. Second, if you aim for Level 4 or higher, outline question 54 before you write, because corrections on grid paper are slow. If questions 51 to 53 go quickly, you can give question 54 up to 30 minutes, the longer plan used on the TOPIK 54 essay page. On the paper test, listening and writing share one 110-minute period, so the writing clock starts when the listening audio ends.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Is TOPIK writing handwritten?

On the paper test, yes. You write all four answers by hand on a separate answer sheet, and questions 53 and 54 go on manuscript grid paper ( 원고지 ), one syllable per square. On the computer-based TOPIK IBT, you type the answers in Korean instead. Check which format your test centre offers.

What happens if my answer is too short?

You will usually lose points. Questions 53 and 54 state a length, 200-300 and 600-700 characters, and an answer well below it cannot fully complete the task, which is one of the marking criteria. The exact deduction is not published for candidates, so aim for the middle of the range and check the current guide on topik.go.kr.

How is TOPIK writing marked?

Trained raters mark the answers by hand against published criteria. For questions 51 and 52 they check whether each blank fits the content and the style of the text. For questions 53 and 54 the criteria commonly published cover content and task completion, organisation, and language use, including grammar, vocabulary and the written style.

Does TOPIK I have writing?

No. TOPIK I has listening and reading only, and every question is multiple choice. Writing appears only on TOPIK II, the paper for levels 3 to 6. If you plan to move from TOPIK I to TOPIK II, start practising questions 51 and 52 a few months before you switch.
